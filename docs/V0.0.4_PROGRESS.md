## Current frozen execution boundary

M01 completion means all new V0.0.4 terrain-process code must use canonical planet/body-space position and a physical-meter sample footprint at public physical-sampling boundaries. Cube/tile/clipmap coordinates remain permitted for page addressing, storage and rendering, but they are not procedural terrain coordinates.

M02 completion adds a second frozen boundary: intrinsic rock physics is authored through `GeologicalMaterial` records owned by `TerrainGeology`. Biomes may later modify protection/moisture/process coefficients but cannot redefine rock hardness, cohesion or intrinsic erodibility. GPU rock tables are derived from CPU authority and are invalidated by the geological material library revision.

M03 completion adds the virtual substrate-placement boundary. `StratigraphyProfile` references M02 `RockTypeId` values and describes finite conceptual layers above an infinite basement material. Terrain processes change the current exposed physical depth; they do not rewrite stratigraphy. Fold, tilt and warp are evaluated from canonical body-space position and therefore do not reset on cube-face, tile or clipmap boundaries. Conceptual layer count affects profile descriptors only, not per-texel/per-voxel terrain storage.

M04 completion adds the permanent authored-terrain authority boundary. `TerrainConstraintSet` persists authored height, gradient, uplift, material, protection and drainage intent through canonical body-space point/brush/spline/polygon/raster primitives. Generated terrain pages are never authority. Protection is a process coefficient (`erosionAllowance = 1 - protection`), so authored landforms can remain recognizable without silently disabling erosion. Material constraints reference M02 rock identity rather than redefining substrate physics.

M05 completion adds the macro-uplift boundary. Tectonic convergence/divergence/hotspot forcing and deterministic long-wavelength body-space distortion combine with M04 uplift authority to produce `MacroGeologyField::upliftMeters`. This is forcing for later drainage/stream-power equilibrium, not final mountain height. M04 drainage guidance remains independent and survives uplift edits. M05 revision identity depends only on geology and authoring, so M06 fine-detail changes cannot invalidate planet-wide uplift.

M06 completion adds the derivative procedural-relief boundary. Continental, ridge and valley bands evaluate in canonical body space and return height plus analytic east/north derivatives from the same function. `TerrainSampleFootprint` removes sub-resolution frequencies smoothly without reseeding or rephasing retained bands. Cheap individual octaves are not cached. M05 uplift remains a separate forcing channel for later drainage/stream-power equilibrium.

M07 completion adds the impact-process boundary. Procedural crater identity, placement and size are deterministic body-space geology; authored placements persist as `.orbitimpacts` authority. Runtime crater output is signed relief plus excavation/ejecta/debris/ray process channels, not protected final height, so M08+ erosion and deposition can physically degrade and fill craters.

M08 completion adds the mutable physical material-column boundary. CPU cells own bedrock height/material plus regolith, soil, sand, debris, moisture and scratch process state. Erosion removes debris→sand→soil→regolith before bedrock; bedrock mass uses M02 density, and deposition covers substrate without redefining it. The derived GPU mirror is four UAV texture lanes (`R32F`, `RGBA16F`, `RG16F`, `R16_UINT`) totaling 18 bytes/texel, with explicit Vulkan RHI support and persistent staging uploads.

M09 completion adds the drainage-derived-state boundary. Flow direction, conditioned elevation, drainage area and discharge derive from M08 physical material state plus explicit neighbor halos. Page edges are not implicit outlets, cube-face orientation is remapped geometrically, and camera/cache residency cannot alter drainage identity.

M10 completion adds the macro-equilibrium boundary. M05 uplift/subsidence moves geological substrate, M04 height intent is a persistent equilibrium target, M04 protection scales incision, and M02 rock competence controls exposed-bedrock response. M10 re-runs M09 routing as terrain evolves and bakes solved displacement/incision through M08 rather than creating a second authoritative heightfield.

M11 completion adds the local-hydraulic boundary. Water depth, virtual-pipe flux and velocity remain solver process state, while transported sediment now publishes through M14 typed waterborne mass. The scalar suspended-mass channel is compatibility/diagnostic state only. Hydraulic erosion still removes the actual ordered M08 material stack, M02 controls exposed-bedrock resistance, infiltration updates M08 moisture, and deposited material returns through the shared M14→M08 path.

M12 completion adds the gravity-relaxation boundary. Loose materials fail according to their own repose angles and retain material identity during redistribution. Exposed bedrock is excluded from granular smoothing; only M02-dependent fracture failure converts rock to physical M08 talus/debris. That debris is immediately compatible with M14 coarse-debris pickup by later transport processes.

M13 completion adds the wind-process boundary. Coarse M01 tangent-frame wind drives exposure/shadowing, carrying capacity, pickup, reptation, saltation, lee deposition and sand-only slip-face avalanching. Airborne sand/fines and surface reptation now publish through M14 shared media. Saltation/reptation that leave a physical page generate explicit M14 boundary packets rather than disappearing or being pinned to the edge. Physical deposition still mutates M08.

M14 completion freezes the shared mobile-sediment boundary. Mobile material is represented exactly once as typed sand/fines/coarse debris across waterborne, airborne and surface-mobile media. Pickup from M08, deposition back to M08, process-specific bedrock conversion, deposition priority and cross-page edge/corner flux are canonical M14 operations. Process-local GPU/CPU ping-pong buffers are scratch only. The persistent GPU mirror is three float4 lanes (48 bytes/cell), with explicit upload/readback; it is not a second terrain authority.

M15 completion adds the glacier-process boundary. Climate eligibility is a hard process mask, glacier thickness/flow are deterministic derived process state, and only M08 owns eroded physical terrain. Basal incision plus flow-perpendicular lateral erosion produces glacial widening instead of reusing fluvial morphology. All glacially excavated material is classified into M14 sand/fines/coarse debris, transported through M14 surface-mobile state and deposited back to M08 as moraine; no glacier-only sediment authority is permitted.

M16 completion adds the editable-river boundary. M09 remains the catchment/drainage source; M16 derives stable selectable node/segment/basin identities, discharge-scaled channel geometry and basin-local authored centreline evolution. Meanders move the physical channel only inside a bounded M09-derived corridor, cutoffs explicitly preserve abandoned oxbow segment identity, and local constraint revisions invalidate only their target basin. Physical river incision samples the evolved M16 centreline, mutates M08 and publishes removed material through M14 waterborne sediment. The older regional HydrologyGrid/RiverGraph/RiverCarving helpers remain compatibility tooling rather than canonical V0.0.4 authority.

M17 completion adds the coastal-process boundary. Shoreline wet/dry state is solved against the current M08 bed, page edges are explicit closed/neighbor/open-ocean shallow-water boundaries, and directional wave forcing enters through those boundaries instead of a rendered displacement. Surf-zone erosion mutates M08 and publishes typed material through M14 waterborne/surface-mobile transport; low-energy deposition returns through M14 to M08. GPU coastal state is fixed-capacity/resident, and disabling the process leaves physical terrain/sediment unchanged.

M18 completion adds the exposed-surface boundary. One canonical resolver maps the actual M08 topmost physical layer plus the matching M02 substrate sample into ExposedSurfaceState. Loose cover and exposed rock are represented distinctly, process-derived water/snow fields cannot change material identity, and mismatched geology is rejected. Renderer and scatter-facing adapters consume this same resolved state; biome-driven visual material composition remains deferred to M19-M21.

M19 completion adds the biome-definition boundary. Every terrain-bearing rocky body now receives one BiomeService with a deterministic, separately stored BaseBiome that cannot be removed. Optional authored Biome Assets carry real placement/surface/scatter/process rule data, resolved optional weights leave residual coverage to BaseBiome, and stale/deleted biome weights fall back safely. Biome definitions do not own M02 geology; one biome asset can be reused over multiple physical substrates.

Generated terrain remains derived state. Camera motion may change residency/request priority but may not alter physical page identity, procedural seeds, geological material identity, stratigraphic identity, authored terrain constraint identity, macro-uplift identity, procedural relief phase, impact identity, physical material-column authority, drainage identity, hydraulic material accounting, aeolian transport identity, shared sediment identity/boundary flux, glacial climate/ice process identity, stable river graph/basin selection identity, coastal boundary/water-process identity, canonical exposed-surface identity, biome-definition/base-fallback identity, or terrain-process inputs.

## Next implementation target

**M20 — Weighted automatic + authored biome placement**

Implement smooth automatic biome selectors for temperature, moisture, rainfall, elevation, slope, aspect, latitude, continentality, water/coast distance, drainage, soil/sand depth, geology/material, solar exposure, wind exposure, snow persistence and arbitrary user fields. Each biome must support Automatic, Authored and Automatic + Authored modes. Persistent authored masks must combine with Add, Subtract, Replace, Multiply, Min and Max operations; Replace must be able to force a biome contrary to climate without destroying the independently editable procedural rule set.
