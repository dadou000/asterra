## Current frozen execution boundary

M01 completion means all new V0.0.4 terrain-process code must use canonical planet/body-space position and a physical-meter sample footprint at public physical-sampling boundaries. Cube/tile/clipmap coordinates remain permitted for page addressing, storage and rendering, but they are not procedural terrain coordinates.

M02 completion adds a second frozen boundary: intrinsic rock physics is authored through `GeologicalMaterial` records owned by `TerrainGeology`. Biomes may later modify protection/moisture/process coefficients but cannot redefine rock hardness, cohesion or intrinsic erodibility. GPU rock tables are derived from CPU authority and are invalidated by the geological material library revision.

M03 completion adds the virtual substrate-placement boundary. `StratigraphyProfile` references M02 `RockTypeId` values and describes finite conceptual layers above an infinite basement material. Terrain processes change the current exposed physical depth; they do not rewrite stratigraphy. Fold, tilt and warp are evaluated from canonical body-space position and therefore do not reset on cube-face, tile or clipmap boundaries. Conceptual layer count affects profile descriptors only, not per-texel/per-voxel terrain storage.

M04 completion adds the permanent authored-terrain authority boundary. `TerrainConstraintSet` persists authored height, gradient, uplift, material, protection and drainage intent through canonical body-space point/brush/spline/polygon/raster primitives. Generated terrain pages are never authority. Protection is a process coefficient (`erosionAllowance = 1 - protection`), so authored landforms can remain recognizable without silently disabling erosion. Material constraints reference M02 rock identity rather than redefining substrate physics.

Generated terrain remains derived state. Camera motion may change residency/request priority but may not alter physical page identity, procedural seeds, geological material identity, stratigraphic identity, authored terrain constraint identity, or terrain-process inputs.

## Verification

The isolated `Orbit V0.0.4 Terrain` Windows lane builds and runs the M00–M04 contract targets. The M04 completion run passed configure, compilation and filtered CTest coverage including `Orbit.TerrainAuthoredConstraints` together with all prior M00–M03 terrain contracts.

## Next implementation target

**M05 — Macro Geology and Uplift Field**

M05 should consume the M04 uplift/height/gradient constraints instead of creating a second authoring system. Body-wide uplift, ridge belts and basins must remain deterministic in canonical body space, and macro drainage/stream-power coupling should shape hydrologically coherent mountains and valleys rather than treating final mountain height as the only authoritative representation.
