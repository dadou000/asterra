M17 adds a real boundary-aware coastal process rather than a visual shoreline
or a cosmetic coastal-erosion switch.

## Authority boundary

- M08 remains the only physical terrain/material-column authority.
- M14 remains the only persistent mobile-sediment authority.
- M17 water depth, momentum, wet/dry state and shoreline masks are derived
  coastal solver state.
- M17 never owns a second coastal heightfield.
- Disabling the process returns M08 and M14 unchanged.

## Shallow-water solver

`terrain_water::CoastalWaterPage` stores:

- water depth;
- depth-integrated east/south momentum;
- velocity;
- wet/dry state;
- shoreline classification;
- wave/current energy diagnostics.

The CPU reference solver uses a positivity-clamped finite-volume shallow-water
update with hydrostatic reconstruction across changing bed elevation.

Time stepping obeys a CFL bound and applies Manning friction and an explicit
velocity ceiling.

When M08 terrain changes, water depth is resynchronized against the new bed
while preserving the existing free-surface elevation.

## Boundary contract

Every physical page edge is explicit:

- `ClosedWall`;
- `OpenOcean`;
- `Neighbor`.

A supplied boundary contains external bed elevation, external free-surface
elevation and tangent-frame velocity. Empty boundary state means four closed
walls.

Open-ocean boundaries support directional harmonic wave forcing. The forcing
uses the configured wave amplitude, period, phase and local tangent direction;
it enters through the actual shallow-water boundary rather than being added as
a rendered displacement.

Emergent M08 terrain remains dry and blocks propagation.

## Shoreline

Wet/dry classification uses separate wet and dry thresholds.

A cell is shoreline state when its wetness differs from a physical four-neighbor
cell. Therefore the shoreline follows M08 terrain automatically.

Depositing enough physical material to raise a cell above the existing free
surface dries that cell and moves the shoreline without authoring a decal.

## Coastal sediment process

Only configured shallow coastal water participates in morphodynamics.

Surf-zone erosion scales with:

- wave/current energy;
- depth inside the active coastal zone;
- shoreline proximity;
- M02 exposed-material competence;
- M04-style protection input.

Loose M08 material is removed in the canonical physical order.

Exposed bedrock uses M02 hydraulic erodibility, hardness, cohesion and fracture
response to determine mobility.

M17 adds `SedimentSourceProcess::CoastalErosion` to M14. Coastal rock
abrasion creates a sand-rich sand/fines load rather than borrowing the rainfall
hydraulic conversion unchanged.

Freshly mobilized material is split between:

- M14 waterborne suspended transport;
- M14 surface-mobile coastal bedload.

Transport is conservative and velocity directed. Sediment leaving the physical
page is published through the existing explicit M14 boundary-flux accounting.

Low-energy and shoreline cells deposit material through M14's canonical
`DepositToColumn()` path, returning material to M08.

## GPU implementation

`GpuCoastalProcessPage` owns fixed-capacity persistent GPU resources for:

- ping-pong water depth;
- ping-pong horizontal momentum;
- wet mask;
- shoreline mask;
- waterborne transport scratch;
- bedload transport scratch;
- four-edge typed M14 export buffers;
- explicit boundary records;
- geology table;
- optional protection field;
- persistent readback resources.

It mutates the existing M08 and M14 GPU mirrors directly.

Seven Vulkan/DXC compute kernels cover:

1. still-water initialization;
2. finite-volume shallow-water advance;
3. wet/dry + shoreline classification;
4. surf-zone M08 pickup and M14 classification;
5. conservative sediment gather/advection and edge export;
6. low-energy M14 -> M08 deposition;
7. M08 material snapshot/readback.

The GPU API requires a declared maximum expected water depth and rejects a
fixed timestep that violates the configured CFL/depth/velocity stability
envelope. This avoids per-step CPU reductions/readbacks while retaining an
explicit stability contract.

## Acceptance coverage

`Orbit.TerrainCoastalProcess` verifies:

- closed still water conserves volume;
- flat still water does not create spurious current;
- M08 terrain edits move the wet/dry shoreline;
- open-ocean forcing generates real shallow-water motion;
- emergent terrain blocks wave propagation;
- beach sand migrates down-current;
- M14 coastal bedload is transported;
- coupled M08/M14 sediment accounting conserves mass;
- the shoreline remains terrain responsive after morphodynamics;
- disabled M17 leaves M08 and M14 unchanged and performs no hidden water work.

`Orbit.TerrainGpuCoastalShaders` compiles all seven compute kernels through
the production DXC/Vulkan shader compiler.

The M14 regression suite also verifies the dedicated M17 coastal abrasion
classification remains mass conserving and sand rich.
