## M30-01 — Stratigraphy exposure

The validation starts from the authored M03 `continental_shelf.orbitstratigraphy` reference profile and a real M08 `MaterialColumnPage`.

The physical bedrock surface begins 0.5 m below the virtual stratigraphic top in volcanic ash. M08 bedrock erosion then lowers that same physical surface through two conceptual layer boundaries. M03 is sampled at the resulting M08 bedrock height and must expose, in order:

```text
volcanic ash
    ↓ 2 m physical incision
sandstone
    ↓ 6 m further incision
limestone
```

The test also replays the first incision on a second identical material page and requires identical bedrock height, removed mass and resolved stratum identity. This freezes the handoff between mutable M08 physical depth and M03's virtual layer stack without voxelizing the strata or introducing render/clipmap state.

## M30-02 — Bedrock stripping

Case 2 freezes the loose-cover → exposed-bedrock transition in the canonical M08 material column.

The fixture places an exact 1.0 m loose stack over authored volcanic-ash bedrock using binary-exact layer depths:

```text
0.125 m debris
0.125 m sand
0.250 m soil
0.500 m regolith
----------------
1.000 m loose cover
bedrock
```

Validation proceeds in three stages:

1. 0.75 m erosion must remove debris, sand, soil and only the upper 0.25 m of regolith. Bedrock height must remain unchanged and regolith must remain the exposed surface.
2. The final 0.25 m must remove the remaining regolith exactly, expose bedrock, preserve both current/reference bedrock heights and report zero excavated-bedrock mass.
3. Only a subsequent 0.5 m erosion request may lower the M08 physical bedrock surface. That incision must enter mass accounting using the exposed M02 rock density.

The loose-material removal ledger is checked against the page's authoritative per-material densities and cell area. This prevents three regressions at once: erosion skipping the fixed loose stack, exact-cover removal accidentally shaving bedrock, and loose-material stripping being misreported as geological excavation.

## M30-03 — Sediment deposition/burial

Case 3 freezes the canonical M14 mobile-sediment → M08 physical-column burial path required by the final integration gate.

The test begins with genuinely exposed authored bedrock and publishes one typed waterborne M14 load chosen to resolve into binary-exact M08 physical depths:

```text
coarse debris -> 0.125 m M08 debris
sand          -> 0.250 m M08 sand
fines         -> 0.500 m M08 soil
-----------------------------------
total burial     0.875 m
```

`SedimentExchangePage::DepositToColumn()` must transfer the complete load into M08 exactly once, empty the selected mobile inventory, and record the transfer as `mobileToPhysical`. The resulting loose-material mass must equal the original M14 payload.

Burial is not geological mutation: current/reference bedrock heights, M02 rock identity and the sampled M03 virtual stratum must remain unchanged while the physical M08 surface rises by the deposited loose depth. Stripping exactly that 0.875 m cover must then re-expose the same bedrock with zero bedrock incision and the same stratigraphic identity.

This case prevents renderer-only burial, duplicated sediment authority, lost mobile mass and deposition that accidentally rewrites geological substrate.
