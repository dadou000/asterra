## Frozen implementation principles

1. **One lighting model.** RT and non-RT paths never become separate GI systems.
2. **Capability is not budget.** Detecting ray-query support does not automatically add lighting cost.
3. **Emission is radiance.** Emissive materials participate in GI independently from bloom.
4. **One emissive authority, many LODs.** LED texels, emitter tiles, city aggregates and planetary emission are derived representations of the same source.
5. **Body/frame-stable GI identity.** Floating-origin movement is presentation, not lighting authority.
6. **Specialized traces remain valid on RT hardware.** Terrain/planet/heightfield visibility is not forced through triangle BVHs.
7. **Physical HDR precedes presentation.** Exposure/bloom/tone map/LUT/output never feed back into GI.
8. **Eye adaptation has separate photopic, dark and overload state.** Extreme highlights do not drag ordinary exposure indefinitely.
9. **UI is part of implementation.** User-authorable/inspectable features are not complete until Studio can exercise them through the shared command/schema path.
10. **Universal volumetrics share one framework.** Smoke/fog/fire/dust/snow/surface-flow use common domain/source/field/render/cache contracts with specialized solvers where needed.
11. **Near interactive, far cheap.** Volumetric Auto mode may use live surface-aligned/local simulation nearby and coarse/passive/baked representations farther away.
12. **Volume lighting is shared lighting.** Volumes do not own a private sun/GI system.
13. **No placeholder renderer.** Every milestone extends the intended production seams.

## Next implementation target

**M11 — Body-stable radiance clipmap data model**

Define the permanent radiance-cache cell identity and GPU storage representation: body/frame-stable integer addressing, directional diffuse irradiance encoding, explicit per-cell validity/revision/update age, documented bytes-per-cell and level memory cost, plus camera-relative GPU presentation coordinates derived from stable keys. Ordinary GPU-origin movement must not change logical cache identity.

M01-M11 remain subject to the normal Windows/Vulkan build, validation-layer and smoke gates; later milestones must not hide failures in those foundations.
