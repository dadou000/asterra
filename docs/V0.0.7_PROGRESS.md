## Frozen implementation principles

1. **One lighting model.** RT and non-RT paths never become separate GI systems.
2. **Capability is not budget.** Ray-query support does not automatically add lighting cost.
3. **Emission is radiance.** GI participation is independent from bloom/presentation.
4. **One emissive authority, many LODs.** LED texels, emitter tiles, city aggregates and planetary emission are derived representations of one source authority.
5. **Body/frame-stable GI identity.** Floating origin is presentation, not lighting authority.
6. **Specialized visibility remains valid on RT hardware.** Terrain/planet/heightfield queries are not forced through triangle BVHs.
7. **Physical HDR precedes presentation.** Exposure/bloom/tone map/LUT/output never feed back into GI.
8. **Eye adaptation separates photopic, dark and overload state.** Extreme highlights do not drag ordinary exposure indefinitely.
9. **UI is implementation.** User-facing features remain accessible through the unified Studio command/schema path.
10. **Universal volumes share one framework.** Smoke/fog/fire/dust/snow/surface-flow use common domains, fields, rendering and caching.
11. **Near interactive, far cheap.** Auto may use live near-field and coarse/passive/baked far representations.
12. **Volume lighting is shared lighting.** Volumes do not own a private sun/GI system.
13. **No placeholder renderer.** Milestones extend production seams only.

Before tagging/closing 0.0.7, run the exact release commit through:

1. Windows configure/build with `BUILD_TESTING=ON`;
2. complete CTest including `Orbit.V007IntegrationGate`;
3. Vulkan validation layers over the M43 scenario matrix;
4. M44 representative non-RT and RT-capable captures with identical authored settings;
5. M43 GPU image-tolerance captures;
6. SDR and available HDR10 output smoke;
7. destructive save/reopen followed by steady-state GPU convergence comparison;
8. root `Orbit.exe` verification for version `0.0.7` and stamped commit.

Missing hardware evidence must remain a pending validation item rather than being converted into synthetic success.
